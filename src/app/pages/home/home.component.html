About Eco Bin

We are bringing waste management activities into one coordinated digital system. Residents can report waste problems, businesses can arrange collections, operators can manage assigned jobs, and administrators can monitor activities across the entire LGA. With location-based reporting, collection tracking and operational monitoring, we aim to make waste management more organised, transparent and responsive.

Our Vision

To create cleaner, healthier and more sustainable communities across Lagos by transforming how waste is managed, collected and recovered. We envision an environment where every neighbourhood is properly maintained, waste is treated as a resource, and responsible waste management creates lasting economic and environmental value.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable and organised waste management solutions across our target Local Government Area in Lagos. We aim to connect residents, businesses, waste collection operators and recycling partners through an efficient system that improves waste collection, encourages responsible disposal, supports material recovery and creates local employment opportunities.

Our mission is to move beyond simply removing waste by building a sustainable system that keeps communities clean, protects the environment and turns today's waste challenges into tomorrow's opportunities.

Our Intention

This project was initiated to address the growing waste management challenges across Lagos, where improper disposal, irregular collection and unmanaged waste continue to affect neighbourhoods, businesses, public spaces and the quality of everyday life.

Our intention is to establish a coordinated, technology-enabled waste management system that serves an entire Local Government Area. By making waste collection more accessible, improving reporting and monitoring, and connecting recoverable materials to recycling opportunities, we aim to make cleaner communities achievable and sustainable.

Beyond environmental cleanliness, we want to unlock economic opportunities through recycling, local partnerships, employment and the development of a more circular waste economy.
