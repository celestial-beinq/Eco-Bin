About Eco Bin

We are bringing waste management activities into one coordinated digital system. Residents can report waste problems, businesses can arrange collections, operators can manage assigned jobs, and administrators can monitor activities across the entire LGA.

With location-based reporting, collection tracking and operational monitoring, we aim to make waste management more organised, transparent and responsive.

Our Vision

To create cleaner, healthier and more sustainable communities across Lagos by transforming how waste is managed, collected and recovered. We envision an environment where every neighbourhood is properly maintained, waste is treated as a resource, and responsible waste management creates lasting economic and environmental value.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, organised waste management across our LGA by connecting residents, businesses, collection operators, and recyclers into an efficient network—improving collection, promoting responsible disposal, and unlocking local economic opportunities through recycling.
